Our Services

End-to-End Automation Solutions

From special-purpose machines to fully integrated SCADA systems, we deliver precision engineering solutions tailored to your production needs.

Special Purpose Machines

Custom-designed SPMs for assembly, testing, and inspection — engineered to maximise throughput and repeatability on your production floor.

PLC / HMI Programming

Expert programming across Siemens S7/TIA, Allen-Bradley, Mitsubishi, Delta, and more — delivering reliable, maintainable control logic.

SCADA Systems

Real-time monitoring and supervisory control systems with intuitive dashboards, alarming, data logging, and remote access capabilities.

Electrical Control Panels

Design, fabrication, and testing of MCC, PCC, and automation panels built to IS/IEC standards with full documentation and testing reports.

Troubleshooting & AMC

Rapid diagnostics and resolution for PLC faults, drive failures, sensor issues, and panel breakdowns — minimising costly production downtime.

Laser Cutting Services

High-precision CNC laser cutting for metal fabrication with tight tolerances, fast turnaround, and quality surface finishes for industrial components.
