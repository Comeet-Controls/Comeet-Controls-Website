## Part 1: Meeting Strategy & Presentation Script

### 1.1 Meeting Objectives
1. **Demonstrate Progress & Vision:** Show them the live prototype (`http://localhost:3000` or Vercel staging preview) to build immediate confidence.
2. **Identify Pain Points:** Understand what is currently missing or broken in their existing digital presence.
3. **Collect Authentic Assets:** Acquire high-resolution machine photos, logos, project specifications, and client logos.
4. **Establish Review & Sign-Off Cadence:** Agree on sprint milestones, deliverables, and go-live date.

### 1.2 Recommended 30-Minute Meeting Agenda

| Time | Duration | Topic | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **00 - 05 min** | 5 mins | Welcome & Introduction | Set meeting goals, acknowledge company heritage (CES to Comeet Controls Pvt. Ltd.) |
| **05 - 12 min** | 7 mins | Prototype Walkthrough | Demo the 6 dedicated pages (Home, About, Services, Projects, Quality, Contact) |
| **12 - 22 min** | 10 mins | Discovery Questionnaire | Ask targeted questions on services, projects, client confidentiality, and inquiries |
| **22 - 27 min** | 5 mins | Asset & Credential Requisition | Review list of required photos, brochures, and domain DNS access |
| **27 - 30 min** | 3 mins | Sprint Plan & Next Steps | Agree on Sprint 1 delivery date and primary contact person |

### 1.3 How to Introduce the New Prototype (Pitch Script)
> *"Hello team, thank you for your time today. We’ve analyzed your current website and recognized that Comeet Controls has evolved into a premier turnkey automation partner with deep capabilities in SPMs, test rigs, and control panels.  
> To reflect your true engineering caliber, we have already built a multi-page, ultra-fast Next.js architecture with dedicated tabs for your services, past projects, quality benchmarks, and an automated inquiry form.  
> Today, we want to walk you through this prototype, understand your exact technical requirements, and gather your authentic machine photography and case studies so we can finalize and launch a world-class website that generates high-value industrial inquiries."*

## Part 2: Client Discovery Questionnaire (What to Ask in the Meeting)

Print this section or have it open during your meeting to take notes.

### Section A: Company Identity, Branding & Heritage
- [ ] **Official Company Name & Registration:** Is the legal entity displayed as **Comeet Controls Pvt. Ltd.**? Do we still need to mention "formerly Comeet Engineering Services (CES)" on the About page?
- [ ] **Corporate Logo:** Can you provide the vector source file (`.ai`, `.eps`, `.svg`) or high-resolution PNG of the official Comeet Controls logo (both dark and light background versions)?
- [ ] **Company Tagline:** Is *"Your Industrial Automation Partner"* or *"Precision Automation Engineered for Industry"* the preferred tagline?
- [ ] **Establishment Year & Milestones:** Is 2012 the exact founding year? Are there any key milestones (e.g., expansion into new Chinchwad works, first export project) you want highlighted?
- [ ] **Team & Infrastructure:** What is your current team size (engineers, technicians, CAD designers)? What is the shop floor area in Sukhwani Fairview / Chinchwad works?

---

### Section B: Services & Technical Capabilities
- [ ] **Core Offerings Validation:** We have categorized your services into 6 pillars:
  1. *Special Purpose Machines (SPMs)*
  2. *PLC & HMI Programming*
  3. *SCADA & Industrial IoT Development*
  4. *Electrical Control Panels (PCC / MCC / VFD)*
  5. *Troubleshooting, Retrofitting & AMC*
  6. *Laser Cutting & Component Distribution*  
  *Are there any new services you want to add (e.g., Machine Vision, Robotics, Pneumatic/Hydraulic overhauls)?*
- [ ] **Hardware Ecosystem & Vendor Affiliations:**
  - Which PLC/HMI platforms do you work with most frequently? (Siemens, Rockwell/Allen Bradley, Mitsubishi, Schneider, Delta, Omron, ABB)?
  - Are you an authorized System Integrator (SI) or channel partner for any brand?
- [ ] **Downloadable Collateral:** Do you have PDF company brochures, machine catalogs, or service line sheets that clients can download from the website?

---

### Section C: Featured Projects & Case Studies (B2B Trust)
- [ ] **Past Project Highlights:** We featured 6 flagship projects from your history:
  1. *Transmission Component Test Rig*
  2. *Precision Multi-Material Rewinding Line*
  3. *Complete End-of-Line (EOL) Assembly Line*
  4. *High-Speed Paper Sheeter Line*
  5. *Hydraulic Oil Pump Performance Rig*
  6. *NVH Acoustic & Vibration System Integration*  
  *Are these still your proudest projects, or do you have more recent/larger machines deployed that we should feature instead?*
- [ ] **Client Confidentiality & NDA Guidelines:**
  - Can we name client companies on the website (e.g. Tata Motors, Mahindra, Bajaj, Bharat Forge, SKF)?
  - Or should we use anonymized descriptions (e.g. *"Leading Tier-1 Automotive Transmission Manufacturer"*)?
- [ ] **Machine Photography & Videos:**
  - Do you have actual photos of completed machines on the shop floor or client site?
  - Do you have short 10–30 second video clips of machines in operation (e.g. rewinding, cutting, or test rig indexing)? Embedded videos dramatically increase industrial client engagement.

- [ ] **Formal Certifications:** Does Comeet Controls have ISO 9001:2015, CE compliance, or specific panel test certifications (e.g. CPRI short-circuit tested panels)?

- [ ] **Customer Testimonials:** Can we feature 2–3 quotes or recommendations from key clients or plant managers?

### 🔹 Sprint 1: Discovery, Asset Intake & Feedback Incorporation
* **Duration:** 3 Days (Immediately after discovery meeting)
* **Goal:** Lock in client feedback, collect media assets, and finalize technical scope.
* **Key Deliverables:**
  - [ ] Meeting notes synthesized into revised requirements document.
  - [ ] Receive high-res logo, real machine photos, and company brochure from client.
  - [ ] Adjust page copy based on exact services and machines confirmed during the meeting.
  - [ ] Update team information, facility address, and official phone numbers.
* **Client Sign-off Gate:** Approved site map, updated content outline, and asset handoff.

---

### 🔹 Sprint 2: Content Enhancement & Real Media Integration
* **Duration:** 5 Days
* **Goal:** Replace placeholder imagery with authentic factory and machine assets; polish typography and micro-interactions.
* **Key Deliverables:**
  - [ ] Optimize client machine photos with Next.js image compression (WebP format for lightning-fast loading).
  - [ ] Build dedicated Case Study modals or detailed specification drawers on `/projects`.
  - [ ] Integrate downloadable Company Brochure (`.pdf`) on `/about` and `/services`.
  - [ ] Add direct WhatsApp for Business quick-connect floating button.
  - [ ] Embed interactive Google Maps location for Pune facility on `/contact`.
* **Client Sign-off Gate:** Preview milestone on Vercel preview URL; client reviews imagery and text.

---

### 🔹 Sprint 3: Backend Workflow, Security & Form Integration
* **Duration:** 5 Days
* **Goal:** Configure live email routing, test anti-spam layers, and optimize SEO metadata.
* **Key Deliverables:**
  - [ ] Link production SMTP credentials (`sales@comeetindia.com` via Gmail Workspace or corporate mail).
  - [ ] Test inquiry delivery to client's actual inbox with formatted HTML email template.
  - [ ] Verify IP rate-limiting (max 5 requests/15 min) and invisible honeypot anti-spam trap.
  - [ ] Add optional file attachment capability (if client requests drawing uploads with RFQs).
  - [ ] Implement OpenGraph social share cards and rich snippets schema (`LocalBusiness` & `Organization` JSON-LD).
* **Client Sign-off Gate:** Test inquiry submitted from client's phone; confirmed arrival in client's inbox.

---

### 🔹 Sprint 4: Cross-Device QA, Domain DNS Cutover & Go-Live
* **Duration:** 4 Days
* **Goal:** Final quality assurance, DNS configuration, and launch on `comeetindia.com`.
* **Key Deliverables:**
  - [ ] Comprehensive cross-browser and mobile device audit (iOS Safari, Android Chrome, Windows, Mac).
  - [ ] Run Google Lighthouse audit (target 90+ performance, accessibility, best practices, SEO).
  - [ ] Add DNS A and CNAME records in domain registrar (`comeetindia.com` -> Vercel).
  - [ ] Verify SSL certificate generation (`https://www.comeetindia.com`).
  - [ ] Submit sitemap (`/sitemap.xml`) to Google Search Console for search indexing.
  - [ ] Deliver project documentation and maintenance guide to client.
* **Client Sign-off Gate:** Official launch sign-off; website accessible live on custom domain.

## Part 5: Quick Reference FAQ for Client Questions

| Client Might Ask... | Recommended Answer |
|---|---|
| *"Will we have to pay monthly hosting fees?"* | **No.** The entire website runs serverless on Vercel's global CDN at **₹0/month**. The only recurring cost is their normal annual domain renewal (~₹800/year). |
| *"Can we update text or projects in the future?"* | **Yes.** The codebase is cleanly structured in modular React components. We can either manage updates under an AMC, or connect a headless CMS (like Sanity or Strapi) if they want non-technical staff to edit content. |
| *"What happens to our old website during the switch?"* | There is **zero downtime**. The new website is built and tested completely on a staging URL. When ready, the DNS switch takes only minutes. |
| *"Is the website secure from spam or hacking?"* | **Yes.** There is no vulnerable WordPress database to be hacked. The contact form includes automated IP rate-limiting, an invisible honeypot bot trap, and HTML sanitization. |
